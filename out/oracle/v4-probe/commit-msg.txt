v4: per-segment speed and fade in/out, proven against the app

The writer gains two keys that change how a clip plays rather than what it
shows, and both were derived from the app's behaviour instead of guessed:

* speed: VideoPad reads a clip's in/out as *speed-scaled* positions into the
  media (source = (in + t) * speed). A probe with in=1000, out=4000, speed=2
  came back with out clamped to 3011 (mediaDuration/speed) and the exported
  first frame reading source 00:00:02.000, which is in*s, not in. The writer
  now divides in/out by the speed, lays out offsets from the scaled lengths,
  and rejects a segment whose window would need out > mediaDuration/speed.
* fadeIn/fadeOut: fadeinduration on the trackclip is reset to 0 by the app, so
  a fade is an effect-chain keyframe group instead - effect 28 ("fade") with
  fractional keyframe positions and 0-100 opacity. The shipped presets only
  ramp across the whole clip, so partial fades are authored as keyframes.

examples/cut5-cadence.json exercises 2x / 1x / 0.5x / 2x-with-mirror and three
fades; measured 13.534 s vs 13500 (34 ms), speed verified by frame MAE against
the burnt-in timecode (0.69 vs 7.48 at 2x, 1.67 vs 9.05 for the scaled in),
fades verified by luma ramps, and the autosave round-trip differs only in the
thumbnail cache. All four earlier examples still reproduce byte-identically.

Also in this change:

* tools/Open-Project.ps1: find the checksum prompt by its [Try Load] button
  rather than by window name (a stacked prompt has no name), which is what made
  it answer [Cancel] and silently abandon the load; report the crash-recovery
  prompt instead of burning the timeout on it.
* tools/Compare-Vpj.ps1: field-level .vpj differ keyed by handle, promoting the
  ad-hoc probe script - the check behind every "round-tripped unchanged" claim.
* docs: speed/fade semantics and the audio dead ends (voll/volr, effectchain
  fadepoints and effect 28 are all inert for audio), mirror's default direction
  is a half mirror rather than a flip, plus v4 evidence and open items.
